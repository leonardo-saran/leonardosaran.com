Hello, and welcome to my website!

My name is Leonardo. I am currently a municipal government employee, a stable career I chose to leave behind in pursuit of technology.


## From childhood curiosity to MMORPG servers

I have been interested in this area since I was a child. I have always had a natural passion for computers and everything related to them, to the point where people close to me would count on me to solve related problems.

Back in my teens, I already knew how to reinstall operating systems and fine-tune their performance; I even did my first deploy with a private MMORPG server:

- dedicated hosting;
- a website with working registration;
- a monetization method.

I wrapped up the project by selling the finished server to a Game Master (GM).


## Law, government exams, and a detour through web indexing

Although I had shown this talent since childhood, I chose, due to indirect family influence, the more conservative path and went to study Law at the Pontifical Catholic University of Paraná (PUC-PR). This route bore good fruit: I built a solid legal foundation and was approved in several highly competitive government examinations, in which, alongside my law coursework, I had to master Grammar, Logic, Accounting, and other fields of knowledge.

While studying for those exams, I simultaneously worked as a Search Engine Evaluator at Lionbridge, which would later become TELUS International. It was an excellent experience, one that helped me understand how search engine indexing worked.


## Rediscovering technology

With the advent of artificial intelligence, the flame of technology grew even stronger. The first time I came into contact with ChatGPT, I was amazed by what was happening and stayed up all night exploring the possibilities. Over time, I consumed a lot of quality content on the subject and developed solutions for personal problems.

Before I realized it, I was spending more time consuming tech content than preparing for my government examinations. Fortunately, I had already passed several of those examinations. So I decided to embrace the talent I had been given and take the risk of working with something for which I have real affinity and genuine interest.


## Toward Software Engineering

However, I consider it crazy and reckless to let AI agents do everything autonomously, without even knowing what is going on. That is why I decided to enroll in a postgraduate program to learn the technique behind the code. Today, I am a student in the Software Engineering postgraduate program at the Pontifical Catholic University of Rio de Janeiro (PUC-Rio), one of Brazil's most respected universities, where I will share my journey and portfolio.


## A living portfolio

Here, I will document my journey through the program, the projects I will develop, and the deliverables of this transition, building a complete record of my technical evolution. My goal is for this site to work as **a living portfolio**: every study, project, and achievement will be registered and accessible to anyone who wants to know my work in detail.

Feel free to reach out!
