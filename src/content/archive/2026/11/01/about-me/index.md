Hello, and welcome to my website!

My name is Leonardo. I have been a technology enthusiast since childhood, and I am continuously learning and exploring.

## Where It All Began

I have been interested in this field since I was a child. I have always had a natural passion for computers and everything surrounding them, to the point that people close to me would often turn to me for help solving real-world problems.

During my teenage years, I already knew how to format computers and optimize systems. In fact, at around 13 years old, I deployed a private MMORPG server with its own website, hosted on a dedicated server and integrated with a SQL database. I eventually wrapped up the project by selling the fully functional server to a Game Master (GM) on the team.

## A Change of Direction

Although I had shown this aptitude since childhood, I chose a more conventional path, partly due to indirect family influence, and pursued a Law degree at the Pontifical Catholic University of Paraná (PUCPR).

That path also brought valuable experiences: I built a solid legal foundation, passed several public service examinations, and had to develop knowledge in Portuguese, Logic, Accounting, and other fields.

While preparing for these examinations, I also worked as a Search Engine Evaluator at Lionbridge, a company that later became part of TELUS International. It was an excellent experience that helped me understand how search engine indexing worked.

## Reconnecting with Technology

With the advancement of artificial intelligence, my passion for technology grew even stronger. The first time I interacted with ChatGPT, I was amazed by what was happening and stayed up all night exploring its possibilities. Over time, I consumed a great deal of content on the subject and developed solutions to personal problems.

Before I knew it, I was spending more time consuming tech content than studying for my examinations. Fortunately, I had already achieved a certain level of success and secured several approvals. So, I decided to embrace an aptitude that had always been there and take a chance on pursuing something I genuinely connect with and have a real interest in.

Although starting a professional career precisely when artificial intelligence has become so advanced may seem contradictory, I consider it reckless to let AI agents execute everything autonomously without even understanding what is happening behind the scenes. That is why I decided to learn the technical foundations behind the code.

Currently, I am working to develop a solid conceptual foundation through self-directed learning. In 2027, I will begin a Bachelor's degree in Computer Science to obtain a formal academic qualification, complementing my independent learning.

## A Living Portfolio

Here, I will document my journey, the projects I develop, and the work I complete, building a comprehensive record of my technical growth.

My goal is for this website to serve as **a living portfolio**: every study, project, and achievement will be documented and accessible to anyone interested in exploring my work in detail.
