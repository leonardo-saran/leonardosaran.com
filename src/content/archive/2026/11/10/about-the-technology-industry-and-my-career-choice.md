title: "About the Technology Industry and My Career Choice"

After spending some time exploring the current tech job market, I've realized that the **technology industry is incredibly broad**, offering many different disciplines and career paths to explore. As a beginner, it's easy to feel overwhelmed by all the possibilities.

I've identified five main areas: software engineering, cybersecurity, infrastructure, data, and product management. Having a broad understanding of these fields can help me make better decisions throughout my career. So, let's take a brief look at each one.

1. **Software Engineering** is primarily about building software and solving technical problems through programming, architecture, algorithms, and software design.
2. **Cybersecurity** focuses on protecting systems, applications, networks, and information against threats and vulnerabilities.
3. **Infrastructure** is concerned with building and maintaining the environments where applications and services run.
4. **Data** involves collecting, processing, analyzing, and interpreting information to support decision-making and build intelligent systems.
5. **Product Management** involves understanding user needs and translating business problems into valuable technology solutions.

I've decided that **software engineering is my starting point** because I see it as a foundation for understanding computer science and how technology works. I also enjoy building solutions to real-world problems and helping people through technology.

I have to admit that **cybersecurity is the field that currently interests me the most**. However, I recognize that specializing in this area is a *mid-to-long-term* goal, as it requires a solid understanding of the systems I'm trying to protect. This means learning how software, operating systems, networks, and infrastructure work, as well as understanding how those systems can fail.

I don't expect to become an expert in every area of IT, nor do I think I need to choose my final specialization right now. My plan is simple: understand the bigger picture, build strong fundamentals, and specialize with intention.

Regardless of which path I eventually choose, I'm absolutely certain about two things: I want to continue **improving my English** and **pursue remote opportunities with international companies**. My English is already at a reasonable level, especially for reading and listening. However, I still have room for improvement, particularly in writing and speaking. Developing these skills is an important personal and professional goal, as I want to feel confident communicating in an international work environment.
