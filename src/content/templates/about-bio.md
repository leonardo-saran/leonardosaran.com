Template About-page bio. Copy this file to `src/content/about/index.md` for English, or to `src/content/about/index.{lang}.md` (e.g. `index.pt.md`) for the second language: then replace with your own story. The `{lang}` code is declared as `lang.code` in `src/content/settings.txt`.

Both files are optional and there is no fallback: the About page shows your bio only in the languages in which it exists.

Write a few paragraphs in first person:

- Who you are, in one sentence
- What you're working on now
- A link or two: your email, your GitHub, your LinkedIn
